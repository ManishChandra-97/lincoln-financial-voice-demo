# General distribution questions

Retirement distributions may have income-tax consequences and an additional early-distribution tax. Exceptions depend on the circumstances and account type. A tax exception does not determine whether a specific plan permits a withdrawal.
Do not provide personalized tax advice or decide eligibility.
For specific eligibility, amounts, tax calculations, loan terms, balances, fees, document types, or exact processing deadlines, say the available information is insufficient and suggest speaking with a qualified specialist.
